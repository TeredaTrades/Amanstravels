Addis Ababa to Dubai: Visa & Logistics Notes

What to check before you go, what to pack, and how the route actually went.

This trip is the one behind the flight clips and the Dubai pin on the map — Bole International in Addis Ababa to Dubai and back. This post is the practical companion: visa basics to check before booking, a packing list, and notes on how the route itself went.

Visa rules change often and vary by nationality and passport. The notes below are general starting points, not a substitute for checking official sources close to your travel date: the UAE's Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs & Port Security (ICP) for UAE entry, and the Ethiopian e-Visa portal for Ethiopia entry/exit questions. Always confirm with the airline and official government sites before booking anything non-refundable.

Visa & Entry — what to check

Regardless of nationality, a few things are worth confirming early:

  • Whether your passport qualifies for visa-on-arrival, an eVisa, or requires a visa arranged in advance — this depends entirely on your nationality, so check the official UAE (ICP) site for your specific passport.
  • Passport validity: most UAE entry rules require at least 6 months of validity remaining from your entry date — a common trip-planning mistake is not checking this until the airport.
  • Whether your visa is single-entry or multiple-entry, if there's any chance of a short side trip or re-entry.
  • Proof of onward/return travel and accommodation — commonly requested even when a formal visa isn't required in advance.
  • Travel insurance requirements, which have become more commonly requested for UAE entry.
