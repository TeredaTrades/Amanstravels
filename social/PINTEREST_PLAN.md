# Pinterest Plan — Aman's Travels

Starter batch: 4 boards, 5 pins, built from content that already exists on
the site (both blog posts + the two documented destinations + a general
overview). No Pinterest account exists yet — this is the plan plus the
ready-to-upload assets; account creation and actual pinning are manual
(need Aman's own email/login).

Images are in `social/pins/` (1000x1500, PNG, generated with
`scripts/generate_pin.py` — see that file's docstring to regenerate or
make new ones). No URL is printed on the images themselves since the site
doesn't have a stable custom domain yet; the destination link is set when
creating each pin on Pinterest, not baked into the image.

## Board structure

1. **Aman's Travels — The Journey** — general/overview board. Where a
   broad "who is this and what's the site" pin lives, and where new
   general-interest pins go if they don't fit a destination-specific or
   practical board. Board description: "Photos, videos, and honest notes
   from a small group of friends' trips so far — Colombia, Belgium,
   Macao, Spain, Ethiopia, the UAE, and South Korea."
2. **Dubai, UAE Travel** — destination-specific highlights from Dubai.
3. **Seoul, South Korea Travel** — destination-specific highlights from
   Seoul.
4. **Travel Costs & Trip Planning** — the practical/logistics content
   (cost breakdowns, visa notes) that isn't tied to one destination's
   highlight reel. This is the board to keep feeding as new
   cost-breakdown or logistics posts get published for future trips.

As new destinations get real content, they each get their own board the
same way Dubai and Seoul did, rather than lumping everything into one
board — Pinterest's own search/recommendation system favors focused
boards over a single catch-all.

## What's next once the account exists

  the site) and the 4 boards above.
